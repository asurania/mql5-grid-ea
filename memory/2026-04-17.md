## Session-end managed close and liquidation

- Three-phase session-end behavior:
  - `SESSION_ACTIVE`: normal trading (new entries, expansion, TP)
  - `SESSION_MANAGED_CLOSE`: no new baskets, existing baskets can still expand and manage TP (mirrors legacy MQL4 `managed_closing_of_trades`)
  - `SESSION_LIQUIDATE`: force close all positions for the pair
  - `SESSION_CLOSED`: no trading at all until next session
- New files: `SessionManager.mqh` (session state evaluation), `Types.mqh` updated with `SessionState` enum.
- New config inputs in `Config.mqh`: `InpSessionManagedClose` (bool, default true), `InpManagedCloseMinutesBeforeEnd` (30), `InpLiquidateMinutesBeforeEnd` (10), `InpNyCloseHourUTC` (21), `InpNyCloseMinuteUTC` (0).
